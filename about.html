Why Djibouti?

Djibouti sits at the Horn of Africa on the Bab-el-Mandeb strait, one of the busiest shipping lanes in the world. French and Arabic are the official languages, and the Djiboutian Franc (DJF) is the currency.

Its landscape is dramatic: Lake Assal lies about 155 metres below sea level, volcanic plains stretch inland and a long coastline runs along the Gulf of Aden and Red Sea. It is a country made for motorcycles.
